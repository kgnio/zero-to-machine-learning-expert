
STAGE 07 - PROBABILITY AND STATISTICS

Descriptive Statistics:
Methods used to summarize and describe data.

Population:
The complete set of observations of interest.

Sample:
A subset of observations selected from a population.

Mean:
The arithmetic average of numerical values.

Variance:
A measure of squared deviations from the mean.

Standard Deviation:
The square root of variance.

Range:
The difference between maximum and minimum values.

Probability:
A numerical measure of how likely an event is.

Sample Space:
The set of all possible outcomes of a random experiment.

Event:
A set of outcomes from a random experiment.

Conditional Probability:
The probability of an event given another event.

Random Variable:
A variable whose value depends on a random outcome.

Probability Distribution:
A description of how probabilities are assigned to outcomes.

Discrete Distribution:
A probability distribution over countable outcomes.

Continuous Distribution:
A probability distribution over continuous values.

Bernoulli Distribution:
A distribution with two possible outcomes.

Normal Distribution:
A symmetric, bell-shaped probability distribution.

Expected Value:
The probability-weighted average of a random variable.

Sampling:
The process of selecting observations from a population.

Sampling Distribution:
The probability distribution of a sample statistic.

Central Limit Theorem:
A theorem describing the approximate normality of sample means under suitable conditions.

Random Seed:
A value used to initialize a reproducible pseudorandom sequence.

Statistical Simulation:
The use of generated data to investigate statistical behavior.
